# Sql-project

## Sale_store_analysis
A retail store chain tracks daily sales transaction,including order details, customer info, product category, order item and order status. The business wants to optimize operation, improve customer experience, and incerase profibility using data driven decision.

## Problem statement

    . Clean the dataset by handling duplicate transactions, NULL values, inconsistent column names, and incorrect data.
 
    . Identify the top five best-selling products by quantity.

    . Analyze products with the highest cancellation rates.
 
    . Determine peak purchasing hours.
 
    . Identify the top five highest-spending customers.
 
    . Evaluate revenue generated by each product category.
 
    . Calculate cancellation and return percentages by category.
 
    . Analyze customer payment preferences and purchasing behavior by age group and gender.
 
    . Track monthly sales and quantity trends.
 
    . Generate actionable business recommendations to improve sales and customer retention.
 
  
##Tools and Technologies

    . SQL Server
 
    . SQL queries for data cleaning and analysis
 
    . CTEs and window functions
 
    . Aggregate functions, CASE statements, and date functions
 
    .Data grouping, sorting, and revenue calculations 
 
 

 ## Final project recommendations
 
 1.Inventory optimization
 
        . Use the top-selling product analysis to maintain stock for high-demand items and reduce stockouts.

 2.Customer retention
 
        . Use customer spending analysis to design loyalty programs and personalized offers.

 3.Revenue growth
 
        . Focus on high-revenue categories and analyze monthly trends to plan promotions.

 4.Fewer cancellations and returns
 
         . Investigate recurring product issues and improve product information and fulfilment.
